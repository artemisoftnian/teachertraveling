A short post per talk doubles as press proof and as search traffic for the event name.
The file name becomes the URL, so `2026-05-keynote-paris.md` is /blog/2026-05-keynote-paris.

## What to include

- One sentence on the promise of the talk.
- Date, city, venue, ticket link.
- A photo from the last time you gave it.
